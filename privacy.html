Luber privacy

This family ride app uses Google sign-in to identify riders and drivers. It receives your Google account email and basic profile, including your name. It does not request access to Gmail, contacts, calendars, or files.

Ride requests include your name, pickup, destination, reason, pickup time, and ride status. These details are stored in this app’s Supabase database. The app’s access rules allow a rider to see their own requests and the assigned driver to see requests assigned to them. Project administrators can access the database to maintain the app.

After a completed ride, the rider may leave a 1–5 star rating and an optional comment. Feedback is stored in Supabase and visible only to that rider and the assigned driver, as well as project administrators maintaining the app.

The location button asks for your permission. Coordinates are entered into the pickup field and sent with your request only when you submit it. The app does not continuously track your location.

Phone alerts are optional. If enabled, the app stores your device’s push subscription to send ride alerts through your browser’s notification service. Alerts contain a new-request message or ride status, without your name, pickup address, destination, or ride reason.

Your sign-in session is stored in this browser. Signing out removes this device’s alert subscription when possible; it does not delete stored ride history. You can also disable notifications in your device settings.

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Google, Supabase, GitHub, and your browser’s push provider process information under their own policies. App source code is public; ride records and server credentials are not included in that code.
